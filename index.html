Model United Nations

Simulate · Debate · Resolve

Step into the shoes of the world's diplomats. Model United Nations (MUN) is a globally recognised academic simulation of the United Nations where students represent countries, debate pressing international issues, and draft resolutions — just as real UN delegates do. It is one of the most powerful platforms for developing leadership, diplomacy, public speaking, and critical thinking in young minds.

At YANF, MUN is not just an event — it is a transformative experience that challenges students to step beyond the classroom and engage with the world as it truly is.

Why participate in MUN
  • Develop confident public speaking and articulation skills
  • Learn to research, analyse, and present complex global issues
  • Practice negotiation, persuasion, and consensus-building
  • Build global awareness and cross-cultural empathy
  • Earn recognition through awards like Best Delegate & Verbal Mention
How MUN works

Each delegate is assigned a country and a committee. They research their country's position on the agenda topic, prepare a position paper, and participate in structured debate sessions guided by the Rules of Procedure (RoP). The debate leads to the drafting and passing of a resolution — a formal document that proposes solutions to the issue at hand.

UN Human Rights Council — promoting and protecting human rights globally.

UNGA

UN General Assembly — the broadest deliberative body of the United Nations.

ECOSOC

Economic and Social Council — development, trade, and social progress.

Crisis Committee

Fast-paced simulation responding to real-time international crises.

What students gain
  • A sharper, more diplomatic way of thinking
  • Confidence to speak in front of large audiences
  • The ability to write structured, policy-level documents
  • An international perspective on global challenges
  • Lifelong skills in negotiation and strategic communication

Parliamentary Debates

Argue · Rebut · Persuade

Where the right argument, delivered at the right moment, wins. Parliamentary Debate at YANF is not just a debate — it is a structured battlefield of ideas, logic, and rhetoric where young minds learn to argue with precision, think on their feet, and defend their stance with conviction. Unlike general debates, Parliamentary Debate follows internationally recognised formats practiced in academic and professional circles across the globe.

Why Parliamentary Debate
  • Master the art of structured argumentation and rebuttal
  • Think critically and respond to opposing views in real time
  • Develop confident, persuasive, and articulate communication
  • Learn to construct logical, evidence-based arguments under pressure
  • Build the skills to speak effectively in any professional or academic setting
Formats we follow

Asian Parliamentary (AP)

One of the most widely practiced formats across Asia: two teams of three — Government (proposition) vs Opposition. Each speaker delivers a structured speech, followed by Points of Information (POIs) and a reply speech that summarises the debate.

  • Teams: 2 (Government vs Opposition)
  • Speakers per team: 3 + 1 Reply Speaker
  • Speeches: 7 min constructive, 4 min reply
  • POIs: between the 1st and 6th minute

British Parliamentary (BP)

One of the most intellectually demanding formats in the world: four teams of two compete simultaneously — Opening Government, Opening Opposition, Closing Government, and Closing Opposition. Each team must advance its own case while answering all other teams.

  • Teams: 4 (two government, two opposition benches)
  • Speakers per team: 2
  • Speeches: 7 minutes each
  • POIs: between the 1st and 6th minute
  • Winner: ranked 1st to 4th by the adjudication panel
What students gain
  • Ability to argue both sides of any issue with equal confidence
  • Improved listening and rapid response skills
  • Sharper logical thinking and analytical reasoning
  • Professional communication skills applicable to any career
  • The courage to speak, disagree, and persuade in any room

Youth Parliament — युवा संसद

Simulate · Legislate · Lead

Experience Indian democracy from the inside. Youth Parliament — युवा संसद at YANF is a powerful simulation of India's most prestigious constitutional and policy-making bodies. Students step into the roles of Members of Parliament, Chief Ministers, Opposition Leaders, Policy Makers, and Advisors — debating real legislative agendas and experiencing Indian democracy first-hand.

More than a simulation, Yuva Sansad is a civics experience that builds informed, responsible, and constitutionally aware citizens — young Indians who don't just understand their democracy, but are prepared to participate in it, strengthen it, and lead it.

Why participate in Yuva Sansad
  • Understand how India's Parliament and policy institutions actually function
  • Build constitutional awareness and civic responsibility
  • Develop public speaking, argumentation, and legislative writing skills
  • Learn the language of policy, governance, and democratic debate
  • Become an aware, responsible citizen of India
How it works

Each participant is assigned a role — ruling party MP, opposition member, minister, or policy advisor — within a simulated house. They research real issues on the agenda, prepare speeches, raise questions, move amendments, and vote on bills. The session follows the actual procedural rules of the respective house, making it as authentic as possible.

Houses & committees simulated at YANF

Lok Sabha

The lower house of India's Parliament — where bills are introduced and debated.

Rajya Sabha

The upper house — representing states and reviewing legislation.

Vidhan Sabha

State Legislative Assembly — simulating state-level governance and local policy.

AIPPM

All India Political Parties Meet — cross-party negotiation on national issues.

NITI Aayog

India's premier policy think tank — crafting solutions for national development.

Press Conference

Students simulate media interactions, policy announcements, and press briefings.

What students gain
  • A deep understanding of Indian constitutional structures
  • The confidence to speak, debate, and hold a position under pressure
  • Awareness of national issues — economic, social, and political
  • Experience in drafting bills, policy briefs, and formal resolutions
  • The mindset of a responsible, informed democratic citizen

Innovation Assembly

Pitch · Invest · Disrupt

Where students stop studying business — and start doing it. Every student has an idea. YANF's Innovation Assembly gives them the stage to prove it. This one-of-a-kind simulation brings the world of entrepreneurship and investment straight to your school — where students pitch real ideas, challenge each other's thinking, and make bold decisions, just like founders and investors do in the real world.

What makes it truly special is the dual experience. One student pitches their vision as an Innovator, while another weighs risks and opportunities as an Investor — and then they switch. By the end of it, every participant has lived both sides of the table. The summit crowns its own Best Entrepreneur and Best Investor, making the experience as exciting as it is educational.

Why Innovation Assembly
  • Experience both sides of the business table — as a founder and as an investor
  • Develop entrepreneurial thinking and creative problem-solving
  • Learn real business vocabulary — pitches, ROI, valuation, market sizing, term sheets
  • Build confidence to present, persuade, and defend ideas under scrutiny
  • Understand how investors evaluate risk, opportunity, and potential
How it works

Phase 1 — The Innovation Pitch

Students assigned as Innovators develop and present a business idea to a panel of student Investors — covering the problem, the solution, target audience, business model, and growth plan. Evaluated on creativity, clarity, feasibility, and handling investor questions.

Phase 2 — The Investment Round

Students assigned as Investors evaluate pitches, ask probing questions, and decide where to allocate their virtual investment portfolio. Evaluated on asking the right questions, assessing risk, identifying opportunity, and making strategic decisions.

Phase 3 — The Summit Awards

The summit crowns the Best Entrepreneur — whose idea won the most investment and admiration — and the Best Investor — whose portfolio choices showed the sharpest business acumen. An experience no textbook could provide.

Business skills students learn
  • Pitching: how to present an idea clearly, confidently, and compellingly
  • Market research: understanding your audience, competition, and industry landscape
  • Valuation: how businesses are valued and what makes them investable
  • ROI & risk: return on investment, risk assessment, and financial thinking
  • Term sheets: the basics of deal-making and investment terms
  • Storytelling: how the best entrepreneurs make investors believe in their vision
